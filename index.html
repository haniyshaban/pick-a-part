About us

Trusted for over three decades

Pick-A-Part started in 1994 in Blantyre as a small business buying and selling automotive spare parts. Over time it grew into wholesale distribution, and today it is one of the largest importers and distributors of spare parts in Malawi.

We import only genuine parts and accessories from trusted manufacturers and suppliers in Germany, South Africa and the UAE. Our knowledgeable, friendly staff are committed to fast, accurate service, so the correct part reaches you on time.
